About Us

We're Building The Future Of App Development

We believe everyone deserves the power to turn their ideas into reality. Apptek was born from that belief — a platform designed to make app creation fast, intuitive, and accessible to all.

Our Mission

To empower businesses of every size with intuitive, powerful tools that remove the barriers between an idea and a fully functional app. We're committed to democratizing technology so that innovation isn't limited by technical expertise or budget. Every entrepreneur, every team, every dreamer should have the means to build something extraordinary — and we're here to make that happen.

Our Vision

We envision a world where anyone can build software — not just engineers. A world where the next great app isn't held back by a lack of coding skills, but is instead driven purely by the strength of the idea behind it. Through Apptek, we're building the bridge between imagination and implementation, making the future of software creation truly inclusive.

Our Story

Apptek started in 2021 in a small garage in Austin, Texas. Our three co-founders — Maya, Jordan, and Leo — were frustrated by how long it took to go from a simple app idea to a working product. They'd watched brilliant entrepreneurs abandon their visions simply because they couldn't afford a development team or navigate the complexity of traditional software building.

So they built the tool they wished existed. What started as a weekend hackathon project quickly grew into something much bigger. Within six months, Apptek had its first 200 beta users. Within a year, that number exploded to 5,000. By 2023, we'd raised our Series A and moved into a real office — no more garage desks. Today, Apptek serves over 12,000 customers across 40 countries, and our team has grown to more than 50 passionate people who share a single goal: making app development effortless for everyone.

We've come a long way from that garage, but the mission hasn't changed. We still wake up every day thinking about how to make the platform faster, simpler, and more powerful. Because somewhere out there, someone has an idea that could change everything — and we want to help them build it.

Our Core Values
